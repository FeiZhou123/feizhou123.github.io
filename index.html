Research Interests

  • Data-Efficient & Generalizable AI
  • Meta-Learning & Few-Shot Learning
  • Foundation Model Adaptation
  • Unified Multimodal Models
  • Embodied AI

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and the Centre for Perceptual and Interactive Intelligence (CPII). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Northwestern Polytechnical University in June 2025, advised by Prof. Lei Zhang and Prof. Yanning Zhang.

My research aims to build data-efficient and generalizable artificial intelligence. I began with meta-learning for rapid generalization from limited data, then moved toward data-efficient foundation-model adaptation for cross-domain deployment. My current work explores unified multimodal models and embodied AI, where generalization must extend across modalities and physical environments.

Collaboration

Prospective collaborators: I am always interested in research collaborations on generalizable learning, foundation-model adaptation, multimodal intelligence, and their applications in specialized domains. Please feel free to contact me by email.
